Frequently asked questions

How do I update my profile?

Open Settings to access your profile editing options. You can update the profile information and photos available to your account.

How do I change my password?

Open your Campusora Settings and use the password change option. Keep your new password private.

What should I do if I cannot access my account?

Check that your login details are correct. If you still cannot access your account, contact support through email or WhatsApp.

What if I have a payment problem?

Contact support and provide the payment reference, amount and a brief explanation of the issue. Never share your password, PIN or OTP.

How can I report a safety concern?

Contact Campusora support and explain what happened. Include relevant usernames or details where possible. Avoid sharing another person's private information publicly.

Can I request account deletion?

Visit Account Management in Settings to access the available account management and deletion request options.

How do I suggest a new feature?

Send your suggestion to Campusora through email or WhatsApp. We welcome ideas that can improve the platform for students and other members.

Important security reminder

Campusora support will never ask you to reveal your password, bank PIN, card PIN or one-time verification code. Do not share these details with anyone.
