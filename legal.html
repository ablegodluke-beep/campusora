Campusora is a social platform designed to help members connect, share content, discover communities and participate in campus-related activities. You agree to use the platform responsibly and lawfully.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account and keeping your login details private. You should provide accurate information and must not impersonate another person.

Certain Campusora features may require payment. Prices, availability and conditions will be displayed where applicable. Payments are subject to the terms presented during the transaction and applicable law.

Campusora may investigate reported activity and take appropriate action against accounts that violate platform rules, subject to applicable law and available platform procedures.

🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Campusora may process information such as:

  • Your name, username and email address.
  • Your selected school, membership role and bio.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, display profiles, operate social features, process transactions, provide support, maintain security and improve Campusora.

Members may request account deletion through Account Management. A 30-day recovery period applies to deletion requests. Certain financial, security or legally required records may need to be retained where permitted or required by applicable law.

🤝 Community Guidelines

1. Respect Everyone

Treat students, alumni and general members with respect regardless of their school, background, opinions or personal differences.

2. No Harassment or Bullying

Threats, targeted harassment, intimidation, hate-based abuse and repeated unwanted behaviour are not acceptable.

3. No Scams or Fraud

Do not use Campusora to deceive people, advertise fraudulent opportunities, steal money or request sensitive financial information dishonestly.

4. Share Appropriate Content

Do not post illegal, exploitative, threatening, sexually abusive or otherwise harmful content. Respect other people's privacy and consent.

5. Be Genuine

Do not impersonate students, institutions, organisations or other individuals. Do not deliberately spread false information about opportunities or events.

6. Respect Intellectual Property

Share only content you own or have permission to use. Give appropriate credit where required.

7. Enforcement

Violations may lead to content removal, account restrictions or other appropriate action. Serious concerns may be escalated for review.

🍪 Cookie Policy

2. Browser Storage

Campusora and its service providers may use cookies or browser storage technologies for functions such as maintaining login sessions, remembering preferences and supporting security.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or clear cookies and website storage through your browser settings. However, removing necessary storage may cause some Campusora features to stop working correctly or require you to sign in again.
