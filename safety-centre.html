CAMPUSORA SAFETY

Safety Centre 🛡️

Your safety matters. Campusora is built to help students, alumni and general members connect in a respectful and welcoming environment.

🛡️

A safer community starts with everyone

Treat other members with respect, protect your personal information and report suspicious activities when you encounter them. Help us make Campusora a better place for everyone.

Protect your account

🔐

Keep your password private

Never share your password with another member. Campusora support will never need your password to assist you.

📧

Protect your personal information

Be careful about sharing your home address, financial details, passwords, verification codes or other sensitive information with strangers.

⚠️

Watch out for suspicious offers

Be cautious of people requesting money, promising unrealistic rewards or asking you to visit suspicious websites.

Community safety

🤝

Respect other members

Harassment, threats, bullying, impersonation and abusive behaviour have no place in the Campusora community.

🚫

Avoid harmful activities

Do not use Campusora to scam people, spread harmful content, threaten members or share another person's private information without permission.

🔎

Check before you trust

Take time to verify people, organisations, opportunities and business offers before sharing information or making payments.

About reporting and blocking

Campusora's reporting and member-blocking controls are part of its platform safety system. These controls will be connected to the relevant profile and content menus during the platform's safety review.

Need help with a safety concern?

If you experience harassment, suspicious activity, account problems or another safety concern, contact the Campusora support team.
